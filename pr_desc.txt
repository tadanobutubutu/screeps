🎯 **What:** Fixed an insecure randomness vulnerability in `MissionSystem.createRandomMission()`.
⚠️ **Risk:** The application was using `Math.random()` to determine mission types and rewards. Since `Math.random()` relies on a predictable PRNG, attackers could potentially predict future missions and rewards, leading to exploitation or farming of the mission system.
🛡️ **Solution:** Replaced `Math.random()` calls with a new `secureRandomInt` function. This function attempts to use Node's `crypto.randomBytes()` to generate cryptographically secure random integers, mitigating predictability. A fallback to `Math.random()` was retained to ensure the system doesn't crash in restricted environments (like the Screeps sandbox) where the `crypto` module might be unavailable.
