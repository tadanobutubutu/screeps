## 2026-02-12 - RoomVisual Accessibility Pattern
**Learning:** High text contrast in Screeps RoomVisuals is critical because text can be displayed over any terrain or structure color. Applying a black stroke (stroke: '#000000', strokeWidth: 0.05) ensures readability across all background states.
**Action:** Use black strokes for all persistent RoomVisual text elements in dashboards and achievement overlays.
