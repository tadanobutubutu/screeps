## 2025-05-22 - [Accessibility & Live Feedback]
**Learning:** The primary brand color #004b73 meets WCAG AAA contrast requirements against a white background, ensuring better readability than #006699 (AA). Additionally, updating relative time indicators at a 1s interval (instead of 10s) provides a more "live" and responsive feel for users monitoring real-time game state.
**Action:** Always prefer #004b73 for brand-colored interactive elements and use high-frequency updates (1s) for "just now" style relative time displays.
