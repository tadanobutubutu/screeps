2024-08-20: Replaced redundant `findPathTo` calls with a shared `PathFinder.search` in `utils.planning.js`. This eliminates CPU overhead caused by recalculating independent paths to multiple sources by manually populating a `CostMatrix` and continually updating the `goals` array with newly planned road segments, effectively creating a Minimal Spanning Tree (MST) of roads. Crucially, when overriding `findPathTo`'s native obstacle avoidance, one must explicitly add constructed walls (`STRUCTURE_WALL`), construction sites (`FIND_CONSTRUCTION_SITES`), sources, and minerals to the `CostMatrix` (with cost 255) to prevent roads from being planned through impassable objects.
